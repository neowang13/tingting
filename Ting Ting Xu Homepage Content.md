# Ting Ting Xu — Homepage Content

## Content direction

**Primary goal:** Make it easy for renters and property owners to take the next step.

**Secondary goal:** Show that Ting Ting also supports buyers, sellers, and property-service clients across Metro Vancouver.

**Tone:** Calm, capable, personal, and direct. The homepage should feel service-led rather than listing-heavy.

**Homepage rule:** Never use “Recently Sold” or “Recently For Sale.” Property cards must come from current data and use the labels **Available Rentals** and **Homes for Sale**. If there are no active listings, show a short enquiry message instead of old inventory.

## Recommended homepage order

1. Hero
2. Find the Service You Need
3. Available Properties — Available Rentals and Homes for Sale visible together, with Rentals given more space
4. Rental & Property Management Spotlight
5. Property Services
6. About Ting Ting
7. Client Reviews
8. Final CTA & Contact

This order gives rentals the strongest visibility while keeping both rental and sale inventory easy to find. The page remains focused by grouping related services and limiting each section to one decision.

## Working voice profile

```text
VOICE PROFILE
=============
Author: Ting Ting Xu
Goal: Build trust and move Metro Vancouver rental, property-management, buying, and selling clients toward a clear next step.
Confidence: Medium — based on the existing website, the new requirements, and the client's direction; final voice should be confirmed with Ting Ting.

Source Set
- Current tingtingxu.ca homepage and About content
- Ting Ting Xu Website Requirements
- Client direction: less busy, service-led, rental-forward

Rhythm
- Short headings, compact paragraphs, and clear service labels.

Compression
- Concise on the homepage; details move to dedicated service pages.

Capitalization
- Conventional title and sentence case.

Parentheticals
- Avoid in public-facing copy unless legally necessary.

Question Use
- Rare; one direct service question is acceptable when it helps visitors self-select.

Claim Style
- Practical and specific. Use only services, credentials, and experience that can be confirmed.

Preferred Moves
- Lead with the client's need.
- Name the next step clearly.
- Use calm, service-oriented language.
- Give rental and property-management actions priority.

Banned Moves
- Unsupported “leading” or “top” claims.
- Hype, market clichés, and urgency pressure.
- Dense lists of equal-weight services.
- Stale “recent” listing labels.

CTA Rules
- One primary action per section.
- Use descriptive actions such as “View Available Rentals” and “Book a Consultation.”
- Keep secondary actions visually quieter.

Channel Notes
- Website: Calm, concise, navigational, and trust-building.
- Email: Not defined.
- Social: Not defined.
```
